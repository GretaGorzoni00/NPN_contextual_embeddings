PCA visualizations of contextual embeddings

This website presents interactive PCA projections of contextual embeddings extracted from a Transformer model in order to qualitatively inspect whether construction-relevant distinctions emerge in the representation space.

Each point corresponds to an instance of an Italian NPN construction or a distractor, depending on the experimental condition. Colors reflect constructional or semantic labels. The visualizations complement probing results by offering a qualitative view of how embeddings are geometrically organized across layers and embedding types.

How to read the visualizations:

  • Points: individual dataset instances.
  • Colors: class labels or semantic labels.
  • Frames / layers: successive hidden layers of the BERT model.
  • Interpretation: visible clustering can suggest distinctions in representational space, but PCA remains a partial projection and should be read together with probing scores.

CMCL framing

The aim is not to treat PCA as proof of linguistic knowledge, but as an exploratory lens on whether the internal geometry of contextual representations could align with linguistically motivated distinctions such as constructional identity and constructional meaning.

UNK · Identification

This view shows PCA projections for the identification task based on [UNK] representations.

Task: Identification Embedding: [UNK]

Why [UNK]?

The [UNK] strategy masks the lexical identity of the preposition, making it possible to test whether the model captures constructional information beyond specific lexical cues.

What to inspect

Look for whether positive and negative instances occupy more distinct regions as layer depth increases, and whether their organization becomes more coherent in later layers.

How it relates to probing

These plots provide a qualitative complement to classifier-based probing: they help assess whether distinctions that are recoverable quantitatively also have visible geometric correlates in the representation space.
